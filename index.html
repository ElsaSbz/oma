About Me

I am a strategic product and growth consultant helping startups and established businesses make better decisions before they scale. Before working independently, I spent more than a decade building and growing digital products within large-scale marketplace environments, including businesses such as Divar and Digikala. These platforms operated with millions of users and complex, fast-moving ecosystems, where product decisions had direct impact on growth, liquidity, and customer value. My work has focused on aligning product strategy, customer needs, and business economics to deliver sustainable, measurable growth rather than short-term traction. I have led initiatives across B2C, retail, SaaS, and B2B2C models, consistently driving double-digit revenue growth, improving prioritization, and helping organizations scale with operational clarity. This includes designing product strategies, introducing data-informed decision frameworks, and enabling cross-functional teams to execute with focus, speed, and shared objectives. My approach combines product thinking, growth strategy, and execution discipline to turn ambiguity into a clear operating model.
